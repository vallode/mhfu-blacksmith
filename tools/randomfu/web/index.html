Randomfu

Monster Hunter Freedom Unite randomizer

    Pick a disc image of Monster Hunter Freedom Unite (EU) or Monster Hunter Portable 2nd G (JP), dumped from your own disc. It stays on your computer: randomfu runs in this page and nothing is uploaded.

    Have a CSO? Decompress it to an ISO first, for example with maxcso or PPSSPP.

    The seed decides every shuffle: the same disc, seed and settings always make the same game. Then start from a preset, or load settings someone shared with you.

    Seed

    Preset

    A preset keeps your seed. You can change anything in the next steps.

    Load shared settings

    These settings change the game's EBOOT, its program, which is encrypted on the disc. randomfu couldn't decrypt yours itself (), so it needs a decrypted copy, which PPSSPP can make for you:

      1. In PPSSPP, open Settings → Tools → Developer tools.
      2. Turn on “Dump Decrypted Eboot.bin on game boot”.
      3. Start the game once. The file is in PSP/SYSTEM/DUMP.

      Check your choices, then randomize. Select a step to change it.

      Share

      Your settings or disc changed since this run. Go back to the review and randomize again to use them.
