What is RandomFU?

RandomFU remixes Monster Hunter Freedom Unite. It shuffles what drops where, what the smithy makes and which monsters wait in each quest, and adds quality-of-life fixes and new challenges the game never had. Every option is yours to pick, and RandomFU writes a new disc image from your own, ready to play in PPSSPP.

Shuffle and randomize

  • Quest rewards What each quest pays, and how often
  • Carves and breaks What every monster gives you
  • Gathering What each gathering point holds
  • Supply boxes What the box in camp holds
  • The smithy Which weapons it makes, from what, with what stats
  • Armor Pieces, skills, defense and slots
  • Monsters Who's in each quest, how big and how tough
  • Weak spots Which elements and parts hurt them most
  • Shops What they sell, and for how much

Quality of life

  • Monster health A gauge for each large monster in your area
  • Damage numbers Every hit, as it lands
  • A better camera Wider, further back, with smooth tilt
  • Send All to Item Box Every reward in one press
  • Less waiting A shorter wait after quests, and a faster start
  • Bestiary and almanac On the bookshelf, so you can find what moved (EU)

All but the bestiary and almanac come from Whetstone, the mods RandomFU is built on.

New challenges

  • Elite monsters Bigger and tougher, for better rewards
  • The Hunter's Pact Take a penalty for a bigger payout
  • Unknown Monster?! Quests that roll a new hunt every time

Balance changes

  • Aggression Monsters that waste less time between attacks
  • Balanced elemental weapons Element that grows with each hit's power

What you need

  • A disc image (.iso) of Monster Hunter Freedom Unite (EU) or Monster Hunter Portable 2nd G (JP), dumped from your own disc
  • This page, in a computer's browser
  • PPSSPP, to play the disc it makes

Your disc never leaves your computer: RandomFU runs in this page and never uploads your files. The same seed and settings always make the same game, so you can share a run with friends.
