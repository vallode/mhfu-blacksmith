Monster Hunter Freedom Unite randomizer

    Choose a disc image of Monster Hunter Freedom Unite (EU) or Monster Hunter Portable 2nd G (JP), dumped from your own disc. It stays on your computer: RandomFU runs in this page and nothing is uploaded.

    Have a CSO? Decompress it to an ISO first, for example with maxcso or PPSSPP.

    The seed decides every random choice: the same disc, seed and settings always make the same game. Then start from a preset, or load settings someone shared with you.

    Seed

    Preset

    Every option does one of two things, and the presets mix them:

    Shuffle
    Moves what's already in the game to new places: rewards, gathering points, the smithy's weapons and armor, shop stock, the monsters in quests. Nothing new is made.
    Randomize
    Rolls new values: material amounts, costs, prices, quest money and time limits, monster size and stats, armor skills, weapon stats.

    Paste a settings string or load a settings file. Replaces every option, and your seed if it has one.

    Happy with these? Skip the options and randomize.

    Check your choices, then randomize. Select a step to change it.

    Share

    Your settings or disc changed since this run. Go back to the review and randomize again to use them.
