Questions

Getting started

Which discs work?

Monster Hunter Freedom Unite (ULES01213), Monster Hunter Portable 2nd G (ULJM05500) and FUC.

Is my disc uploaded anywhere?

No.

What can I play it on?

It's played and tested in PPSSPP. A PSP with custom firmware may work, but hasn't been tried yet.

Playing

What happens to my save?

Back up your save file. That's good advice in general. A randomized run with an existing character isn't the best idea, but it will generally work: your gear will just change drastically. What RandomFU adds to the game is kept in your save file.

Can I change settings once I'm playing?

Some of them. Press START in a quest: the menu's last entry, randomfu, opens a window for the camera, monster health, damage numbers, attack hitboxes and the wait after a quest. Your save keeps what you choose. The shuffles are written into the disc, so changing those means building a new one.

Can I play with friends?

Not together, for now: a RandomFU disc is offline, so it can't play ad hoc. You can still play the same run side by side. Build from the same disc, seed and settings and the quests and rewards match: share the settings string from the Review step.

Please don't take a randomized hunter online on an unmodified disc either (ad hoc, HunterVerse or any other server). Its gear and items came from a different game, and the people you'd hunt with didn't sign up for that. Keep a separate save for RandomFU.

Can a shuffle leave me stuck?

RandomFU guards against the ways a shuffle could make a quest impossible: a delivery whose item can't be found or carved, a slaying goal with too few monsters, a monster starting where it can't stand, health past what the game can hold. Checked over seeds on the EU disc with everything on, none got through. It can't guard against your own choices (a Hunter's Pact penalty, or letting shops and supply boxes drop their essentials) or bad luck. If a quest still can't be finished, report it (below).

Seeds and settings

What's a seed?

The word that every random choice grows from. The same disc, seed and settings always make the same game, so a run can be shared, raced or rebuilt.

How do I share my settings?

The Review step shows a settings string that holds the seed and every option: copy it, or save it as a file. Paste it into the Seed & preset step to load it. A string made by an older version of RandomFU still loads, but warns that its game will come out differently.

What's the spoiler log?

A list of everything that moved: every reward, recipe, monster and shop. It downloads with the disc, and Preview spoiler shows it without building one. Don't read it if you'd rather find out by playing.

Can I have only the quality-of-life changes?

Yes. Pick the QoL only preset: nothing is shuffled or randomized, and it adds a few extras. Turn on the camera, HUD and quest extras you want in the later steps.

More

Something went wrong. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on GitHub, with your settings string and which disc you used. RandomFU is early, and reports from real play help most.

Is it open source?

Yes, under the GPL (licence). It lives alongside the MHFU Blacksmith site, in one repository.
