What is Whetstone?

Whetstone is a collection of quality-of-life changes and feature mods for Monster Hunter Freedom Unite: HUD extensions and menu shortcuts, new armor skills, more aggressive monsters, and more!

Pick the mods you want and Whetstone patches them into your own disc image, ready to play in PPSSPP.

Camera

  • Field of view Up to 1.4 times as wide as the game's
  • Camera distance Up to 1.5 times as far back
  • Smooth tilt The D-pad tilts to any angle (EU)
  • Photo mode Freeze the quest, hide the HUD and move the camera
  • Target camera L turns the camera to a large monster

HUD

  • Monster health A gauge for each large monster in your area
  • Damage numbers Every hit, as it lands
  • Attack hitboxes A faint red shading over attacks that can hit you

Quality of life

  • Send All to Item Box Every reward in one press
  • Improve pips A pip on each weapon you can improve
  • Harvest all The whole farm to the Item Box with △
  • Bulk combine Combine as many as you can carry at once
  • Key quest marks A ★ on the quests that unlock the Urgent Quest
  • Gathering nodes on the map Every gathering, mining and bug point marked
  • Paint stays A painted monster stays on the map all quest
  • Slain and captured The Monster List counts both
  • Quest end wait A shorter wait before the rewards, or none

Monsters and Poogie

  • Elite monsters Now and then a tougher one, for better rewards
  • Aggression Less time between attacks
  • Time out of reach Less flying, digging and leaving mid-fight
  • Element by motion value Heavy hits carry more element
  • Poogie's luck Pet Poogie before a hunt for a little luck

Boot and buttons

  • Faster start Skip the logos and intro, or go straight to the village
  • Button icons Xbox, Nintendo, PlayStation or keyboard buttons
