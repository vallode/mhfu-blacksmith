Questions

Getting started

Which discs work?

Monster Hunter Freedom Unite (ULES01213), Monster Hunter Portable 2nd G (ULJM05500) and FUC. A few mods are for some of them only; their options say which.

Is my disc uploaded anywhere?

No. Whetstone runs in the page and patches a copy of your disc in your browser. The page talks to no server, and counts nothing.

What can I play it on?

PPSSPP.

Playing

What happens to my save?

Back up your save file, as with any mod. Your hunter and items are unchanged; the choices you make in game are kept in your save.

Can I change settings once I'm playing?

Some of them. Press START in a quest: the menu's last entry, Whetstone, opens a framed window in the game's own style for the camera, monster health, damage numbers, attack hitboxes and the wait after a quest. The other mods are written into the disc, so changing those means patching a new one.

Can I play with friends?

Not together: every disc Whetstone makes is offline, so it can't play ad hoc or join a server.

Settings

How do I share my settings?

The Review step shows a settings string, starting WS, that holds every mod: copy it, or save it as a file. Load either in the Preset step.

What's the log?

A list of what went into your disc, mod by mod. It downloads with the disc, and Preview the log first shows it without making one.

How is it related to RandomFU?

RandomFU, the Monster Hunter Freedom Unite randomizer, is built on Whetstone: it has every mod here, beside its shuffles. Use Whetstone for the mods alone.

More

Something went wrong. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on GitHub, with your settings string and which disc you used.

Is it open source?

Yes, under the GPL (licence). It lives alongside the MHFU Blacksmith site, in one repository.
